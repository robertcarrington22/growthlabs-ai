# Lead Gen — Services Batch 1 (Agencies, B2B Consultancies, MSPs)

**Date compiled:** 2026-08-19
**Method:** Public web only — press releases (PRNewswire/PRWeb/EIN/GlobeNewswire), award lists (Inc. 5000 2026, Ad Age Small Agency Awards 2025/2026, MSP 501 2026, 2026 MSPs to Watch), company websites, local business press. No LinkedIn scraping or logged-in data collection. Every claim has a source URL that was fetched or seen in search results.
**ICP filters applied:** US HQ · 10–75 employees · est. $500K–$10M rev · founder/CEO/owner reachable · agency / B2B consultancy / MSP / professional-services boutique.
**Excluded (already tracked):** Focus Lab, Newfangled, Column Five, Mabbly, Bop Design, Sweet Fish Media, Kelser Corp, Single Grain, IMPACT, Kuno Creative, Zen Media, Funnel Clarity — none duplicated below.

**Dropped during vetting (out of range):** WingSwept (85+ staff), DP Solutions (~129 staff), Saylor (~88 staff), Attain Technology (1–10 staff), Elysium Marketing Group (2–10 staff), Hero Marketing Agency (team appears <10).
