# GrowthLabs AI — Data Security & Privacy

**Last updated:** July 2026
**Applies to:** All client data uploaded for Revenue Discovery Audits

---

## What We Receive

When you upload data for your audit, we receive:
- **Transaction records:** Transaction IDs, customer IDs, dates, amounts, service names
- **Customer records:** Company names, industries, acquisition channels, dates
- **Financial summaries:** Revenue totals, margins (if provided)

**We do NOT receive:**
- Personal employee data (names, salaries, SSNs)
- Banking credentials or login information
- Credit card numbers or payment details
- Tax IDs or government identifiers

---

## Who Sees Your Data

Your data is accessible to:
- **Your assigned GrowthLabs AI analyst** — runs the analysis
- **The AI analysis engine** — automated processing (no human reads individual transactions)

**We never:**
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data to train models for other clients
- Publish your data without explicit written permission

---

## Where Your Data Is Stored

- **Processing:** Encrypted cloud servers (US-based)
- **At rest:** Encrypted database storage
- **Analytics outputs:** Anonymized aggregate metrics only (e.g., "average revenue gap across all clients")

---

## How Long We Keep It

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Audit input files (CSVs) | Deleted within **30 days** of audit completion |
| Audit report (output) | **12 months** — accessible via your client portal |
| Anonymized aggregate metrics | **Indefinitely** — no client-identifiable data |

---

## Deletion on Request

You can request deletion of your data at any time:

1. Email **privacy@growthlabs.ai** with your client name
2. We will:
   - Delete all raw input files within **72 hours**
   - Delete your audit report within **5 business days**
   - Confirm in writing when deletion is complete

---

## Compliance

- **US-based:** We operate under US data protection standards
- **CCPA:** If you are a California business, you have the right to know what data we hold and request its deletion
- **No GDPR scope:** We do not target or serve EU businesses at this time
