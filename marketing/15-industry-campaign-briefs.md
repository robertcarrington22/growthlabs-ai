## Campaign 1: Digital Agencies & Marketing Services

### Target Profile
- **Revenue**: $500K–$10M
- **Size**: 10–75 employees
- **Geography**: US (primary hubs: NYC, LA, Chicago, Austin, Atlanta, Denver)
- **Decision maker**: Founder / CEO / Head of Growth
- **Key pain point**: Pricing drift, scope creep, utilization gaps

### Key Research Insights (from strategy-deep-dive.md)
- Typical utilization rate: 55–65% → best-in-class: 75–85% → 10% utilization increase = 15–30% revenue gain
- 20% of projects subsidize the rest (hidden margin leakage)
- Value-based pricing transition can yield 25–50% revenue increase
- Most agencies have no project-level profitability data

### Message Framework
| Stage | Message |
|-------|---------|
| **Hook** | "Your agency is probably leaving 20% of revenue on the table — and it's hiding in plain sight." |
| **Problem** | "Most agencies track revenue but not project-level profitability. You don't know which clients are subsidizing which." |
| **Solution** | "Our AI audit analyzes 3 years of your billing data to find pricing gaps, utilization leaks, and upsell opportunities — in 2 weeks." |
| **Proof** | "We're currently working with 10 founding clients at no cost in exchange for feedback" (D4.3 founding-client offer) |
| **CTA** | "Apply for a founding-client audit →" |

### Content Pillars
1. **"The $50K scope creep problem"** — Short, shareable stat about unbilled work
2. **"Why your agency's utilization rate is a revenue warning light"** — Educational post
3. **"The 3 pricing models every agency needs to know"** — Hourly → Fixed → Value-based
4. **"How to know if you're underpricing your best clients"** — Diagnostic angle

### Channels
- LinkedIn: Direct outreach to agency founders + posts
- MicroConf Connect / Agency Management Institute communities
- Partner referrals via fractional CFOs serving agencies

## Campaign 2: B2B SaaS (SMB-focused)

### Target Profile
- **Revenue**: $1M–$10M ARR
- **Size**: 10–75 employees
- **Geography**: US (primary hubs: SF, NYC, Austin, Boston, Seattle, Denver)
- **Decision maker**: Founder / CEO / Head of Revenue
- **Key pain point**: Churn, pricing tier confusion, low NRR

### Key Research Insights
- Average SMB SaaS NRR: 80–100% → best-in-class: 120%+
- Monthly churn (SMB): 5–10% → best-in-class: <3%
- LTV:CAC ratio: 2:1–3:1 typical → 5:1+ best-in-class
- Free-to-paid conversion: 2–4% typical
- HubSpot case study: freemium → tiered pricing drove 170× growth
