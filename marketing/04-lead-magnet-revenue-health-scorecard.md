# Lead Magnet: Revenue Health Scorecard

## Concept

A free, self-administered diagnostic tool that scores a business across 5 dimensions of revenue health. Users answer ~15 questions and receive an instant score (0–100) plus a prioritized list of revenue gaps to address.

**Goal**: Capture qualified leads (name, email, company, revenue range) by delivering immediate value — actionable insight, not fluff.

**Distribution**: Gated behind email capture on website, promoted in blog posts, shared on LinkedIn.

---

## Scorecard Outline

### Dimension 1: Pricing Health (20 points)

Questions:
1. When did you last conduct a pricing review? (>12 months = 0 pts, 6–12 = 2, <6 = 4)
2. How many distinct pricing tiers/plans do you have? (1 = 4, 2–4 = 3, 5+ = 1, "don't know" = 0)
3. Do you know what % of your clients are on discounted or grandfathered rates? (yes = 4, no = 0)
4. How confident are you that your prices match market rates? (0–4 scale)

### Dimension 2: Churn Risk (20 points)

Questions:
1. What is your monthly churn rate? (<2% = 4, 2–5% = 2, >5% = 0, "don't know" = 1)
2. Do you track churn by client segment? (yes = 4, no = 0, partially = 2)
3. Do you have a churn prediction/early warning system? (yes = 4, partially = 2, no = 0)
4. What's your average client lifetime (months)? (>24 = 4, 12–24 = 2, <12 = 0)

### Dimension 3: Upsell/Expansion (20 points)

Questions:
1. Do you have a systematic upsell program? (yes = 5, occasionally = 2, no = 0)
2. What's your Net Revenue Retention rate? (>110% = 5, 100–110% = 3, <100% = 1, "don't know" = 1)
3. How many of your clients buy more than one service? (>60% = 5, 30–60% = 3, <30% = 1)
4. Do you track expansion revenue separately? (yes = 5, no = 0)

### Dimension 4: Channel Effectiveness (20 points)

Questions:
1. Do you know your CAC by acquisition channel? (yes for all = 5, for some = 3, no = 0)
2. Do you track LTV by acquisition channel? (yes = 5, no = 0)
3. How many channels drive predictable, profitable growth? (3+ = 5, 1–2 = 3, 0 = 1)
4. Do you use multi-touch attribution? (yes = 5, basic last-touch = 2, no = 0)

### Dimension 5: Data Maturity (20 points)

Questions:
1. How integrated are your CRM, billing, and analytics tools? (fully = 5, partially = 3, not at all = 0)
2. How often do you review revenue data outside of standard monthly reports? (weekly = 5, monthly = 3, quarterly or less = 1)
3. Do you currently use any AI/ML tools for revenue analysis? (yes = 5, no = 0)
4. How confident are you that you're capturing 100% of your addressable revenue? (0–5 scale)

### Scoring

| Score | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100 | Revenue Healthy | Fine-tuning recommendations |
| 60–79 | Moderate Gaps | 2–3 high-impact opportunities to pursue |
| 40–59 | Significant Leaks | Priority audit recommended |
| 0–39 | Critical | Full revenue transformation needed |
