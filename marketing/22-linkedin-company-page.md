> **Reality check:** LinkedIn does not offer standalone "business accounts." A company page must be created and administered from a personal profile, and LinkedIn requires that profile to be reasonably established (7+ days old, some connections). The page gives the brand a home; **the outreach and posting still run from the personal profile** — that's also just what works (founder posts massively out-reach company pages).

---

## Create the page (10 minutes)

1. Log in to LinkedIn → click the **"For Business"** grid icon (top right) → **"Create a Company Page"**
2. Choose **Company**
3. Fill the fields below, upload the images, click **Create Page**
4. Then open the page → **Edit Page** and complete the remaining fields
