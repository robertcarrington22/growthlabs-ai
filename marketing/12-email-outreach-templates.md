- **Phase 1**: Cold intro (value-first, no pitch)
- **Phase 2**: Follow-up (social proof, case study)

All templates use the "value-first" approach: lead with insight, not a sales pitch.

## Email Best Practices

- **Personalize**: Always reference their company, industry, or recent news in the first paragraph
- **Keep it short**: 3–5 sentences max for cold outreach
- **Value-first**: Lead with the free scorecard, not a sales call
- **No attachments**: Use links instead (increases deliverability)
- **Track opens**: Optional (use HubSpot or Mailshake when available)
- **Follow LinkedIn connection**: Send email 2–3 days after LinkedIn connection is accepted
