## Rules of engagement

1. **Max 5 connection requests/day** from a new-ish profile — LinkedIn flags bursts.
2. **Never pitch in the connection note.** The note earns the accept; the DM (days later) earns the conversation.
3. Comment on the person's recent post *before* sending the request when possible — warm names get accepted at ~2× the rate.
4. Log every send/accept/reply in `outreach-tracker.csv` the same day.
5. If no reply to the DM after 7 days: one light follow-up ("floating this back up — slots going to [n] remaining"), then stop. No third touch.
