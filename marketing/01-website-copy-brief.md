# Website Copy Brief — GrowthLabs AI

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Voice**: Confident but not arrogant. Data-driven but human. Think "trusted advisor with receipts."
- **Tone**: Authoritative, approachable, action-oriented. Use "you" language. Avoid hype — let data speak.
- **Vocabulary**: Revenue growth, AI-powered analytics, pricing optimization, churn reduction, upsell opportunities, data-backed decisions, revenue gaps.
- **Avoid**: Buzzwords (synergize, leverage, pivot), vague claims ("we'll grow your business"), overly technical jargon.

---

### 3. How It Works (`/how-it-works`)

**Headline**: From data to dollars in three phases.

**Phase 1 — Audit (Week 1–2)**
- Connect your data (CRM, billing, analytics)
- AI engine maps customer segments, pricing tiers, churn signals
- We identify 10–20 revenue opportunities ranked by impact
- Deliverable: Discovery Audit Report

**Phase 2 — Plan (Week 3)**
- We present findings in a strategy session
- Prioritize 3–5 highest-impact initiatives
- Build a 90-day execution roadmap
- Deliverable: Revenue Growth Plan

**Phase 3 — Execute (Ongoing)**
- Monthly implementation support
- Track progress with shared dashboards
- Iterate based on real results
- Deliverable: Monthly Growth Reports

**CTA**: "Start Your Audit →"
