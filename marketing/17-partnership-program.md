# Fractional CFO / Bookkeeper / POS Reseller Partnership Program

## Why This Channel (from the research)

The review identified this as the highest-potential channel for three reasons:
1. **Trust**: CFOs already have the client's confidence and data access
2. **Data access**: The CFO already has the books — the #1 bottleneck is solved
3. **Alignment**: CFOs see revenue problems but can't solve them with AI tools

**Retail extension**: CFOs also see trapped cash on every retail balance sheet. POS resellers (Square/Lightspeed/Shopify consultants) see inventory data daily and have trusted relationships with their merchants.

## Partner Profiles

### Track A: Fractional CFOs & Bookkeepers (Service Business Focus)
- **Role**: Independent fractional CFO, small CPA firm, bookkeeping service (5–50 clients)
- **Client base**: 10–75 employee service businesses ($500K–$10M revenue)
- **Pain point**: Their clients have revenue problems but they lack the tools to diagnose them
- **Motivation**: Additional revenue stream + better client outcomes without more work

### Track B: POS Resellers & Consultants (Retail Focus)
- **Role**: Square/Lightspeed/Shopify/Clover resellers, POS implementation consultants, retail-specialist bookkeepers
- **Client base**: Independent retailers, e-commerce brands, hybrid brick+click ($500K–$10M revenue)
- **Pain point**: Their clients have cash tied up in inventory but don't know how to analyze it
- **Hook**: White-label **Inventory Health Scorecard** they can give their merchants — shows trapped cash using classical retail metrics (GMROI, turns, ABC analysis) that big-box stores use

### Where to Find Partners
- **Fractional CFOs**: Paro.io network, B2B CFO network, local CPA firms, LinkedIn
- **POS Resellers**: Square Partner directory, Shopify Partner directory, Lightspeed Partner network, Clover App Market developers
- **Retail Bookkeepers**: Specialized bookkeeping firms serving e-commerce/retail clients
- **Industry groups**: EO / Vistage (CFO members), Retail-specific accounting groups

## Partnership Model

### What We Offer Partners
| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **White-label Scorecard** | Co-branded Revenue Health Scorecard they can give clients — makes them look data-savvy |
| **Referral fee** | 15–20% of Discovery Audit fee + first 2 months of retainer for any client they refer |
| **Client insight** | Free mini-audit of their own practice (demonstrates value) |
| **Co-marketing** | Joint case studies, co-branded content |
| **No heavy lifting** | We do the analysis, they get the credit |

### What We Need From Partners
- **Introduction** to clients who fit our ICP (10–75 employees, $500K–$10M, service business)
- **Brief qualification** before intro (confirm ICP fit)
- **Data access facilitation** (client handover or introduction)

### Referral Fee Structure
| Referral Source | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Partner refers client who buys Discovery Audit | 15% of audit fee |
| Partner refers client who converts to retainer | 20% of first 2 months retainer |
| Partner refers 3+ clients in a quarter | Bonus: 25% of first 3 months |

## Partner Outreach Sequence

### Step 1: Identify (Week 1–2)
- Search LinkedIn for fractional CFOs, CPAs, bookkeepers in target US cities
- Look for: "fractional CFO for [agency/consulting/SaaS]"
- Target: 20 potential partners in first 30 days

### Step 2: Connect (Week 2–3)
**LinkedIn Connection Request** (290 chars):
> Hi [Name] — I help fractional CFOs add AI-powered revenue analysis to their client offerings. Would love to connect and share a white-label tool your clients would love.

**After-accept Message**:
> Thanks for connecting! I built a Revenue Health Scorecard that analyzes pricing, churn, and upsell opportunities for service businesses. I'm looking for CFO partners who want to offer this to their clients — white-labeled, zero work for you.
> 
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if it's a fit?

### Step 3: Partner Call (Week 3–4)
**Agenda**:
1. Demo the Revenue Health Scorecard (5 min)
2. Show sample audit findings (5 min)
3. Explain the white-label opportunity (5 min)
4. Offer: free mini-audit of their own practice (5 min)
5. Next steps: referral agreement

### Step 4: Onboard (Week 4+)
- Send partner agreement (simple one-pager)
- Provide white-label scorecard link
- Set up referral tracking
- First client introduction → execute Discovery Audit
