# Case Study Framework v2 — GrowthLabs AI

## Updated per External Review Directives

**Critical changes from v1**:
1. **No unearned claims** — All quantified results are aspirational ("what our methodology targets") or attributed to named industry sources. No "we achieved X% improvement" until we have real client data.
2. **Founding-client focus** — First case studies come from founding-client cohort (free audits in exchange for testimonials).
3. **US market** — All examples, benchmarks, and references use US companies and USD.

---

## Case Study Structure

### 1. Client Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Company** | [Name] — [Industry] |
| **Location** | [City, State] |
| **Size** | [N] employees |
| **Revenue** | $[X]M/year |
| **Challenge** | One-line summary |
| **Engagement** | Discovery Audit → [Retainer / One-time] |
| **Timeline** | [X] weeks |

### 2. The Challenge (2–3 paragraphs)

**What to include**:
- The situation before we started
- What the client *thought* the problem was
- What data was available (CRM, billing, analytics)
- Why manual analysis wasn't working

**Example**:
> [Company] came to us because growth had plateaued. They were acquiring new clients steadily, but revenue wasn't keeping pace. The founder suspected pricing was an issue — but with 200+ clients across 3 service lines, nobody had time to audit every relationship.

### 3. Our Approach (2–3 paragraphs)

**What to include**:
- How we analyzed the data (AI engine, what we looked for)
- The patterns that emerged
- Key insights we presented
- Reference the Revenue Health Maturity Model framework

**Example**:
> We connected their CRM, billing system, and project management tools. Our AI engine analyzed every client relationship against 4 dimensions: pricing consistency, churn risk, expansion opportunity, and channel efficiency. 
> 
> What emerged: clients acquired before 2023 were paying 35% less than comparable new clients for the same service scope. We also identified 12 clients whose usage patterns suggested they were ready for an upgrade.

### 4. Results (bulleted, quantified)

**Format**: Bullet points with specific numbers. Label as "What our methodology targets" or "Initial findings" until we have track record.

**Example**:
- **$240K** in annual upsell opportunities identified (based on service usage patterns)
- **22%** pricing consistency gap across client base (clients paying different rates for same service)
- **15%** of clients showed early churn signals (engagement decline, support ticket increase)
- **3-month** expected payback period for the audit investment

**Visual**: Before/after comparison table

| Metric | Before | After (Target) | Change |
|--------|--------|---------------|--------|
| Pricing consistency | 62% | 84% | +22% |
| Monthly upsell revenue | $2K | $12K | +500% |
| At-risk churn (segment) | 15% | 8% | -47% |

### 5. Client Quote

Direct quote from the client. Format:

> *"The most valuable $495 we've spent this year. They found revenue opportunities we'd been walking past for years."*
> 
> — **[Name], [Title] at [Company]**

### 6. What's Next (optional)

Brief note on the client's ongoing engagement or next steps.

## Pre-Client: "Founding Client" Offer Page

### Headline
**Be one of 10 founding clients. Get a full Discovery Audit — free.**

We're launching GrowthLabs AI and looking for 10 US service businesses to be our founding clients.

**What you get:**
- Full AI-powered Discovery Audit (normally $495–$995)
- Personalized revenue growth plan with prioritized recommendations
- 30-minute strategy session to walk through findings

**What we ask in return:**
- Your honest feedback on the audit process and findings
- Permission to publish a case study (anonymized if preferred)
- A testimonial if you're happy with the work

**Who qualifies:**
- US-based service business (agency, consultancy, SaaS, MSP, professional services)
- $500K–$10M annual revenue
- 10–75 employees
- Willing to share 3 years of transaction/billing data

**CTA**: "Apply for Founding Client Program →"

### Distribution
- LinkedIn post (#1 founding-client offer post)
- Email outreach to new US Tier 1 list
- Landing page on website
- Partner referral intro
