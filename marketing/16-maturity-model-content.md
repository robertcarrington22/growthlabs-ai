## Asset 1: "What Stage Is Your Revenue At?" — Interactive Quiz

### Concept
A 10-question quiz that places a prospect in one of the 5 stages, then delivers personalized content + a CTA for the Scorecard.

### Quiz Questions
1. What's your annual revenue range? ($0–$500K / $500K–$2M / $2M–$5M / $5M–$10M / $10M+)
2. How often do you review your pricing? (Never / Once a year / Quarterly / Monthly)
3. Do you know your churn rate by customer segment? (No / Roughly / Yes, precisely)
4. Where do you get most of your revenue data? (Gut feeling / Spreadsheets / CRM dashboards / Automated)
5. How many of your clients have been with you for 2+ years? (<30% / 30–60% / 60–80% / 80%+)
6. Do you know which marketing channel gives you your highest-value clients? (No / Somewhat / Yes)
7. How often do you upsell or cross-sell to existing clients? (Never / Occasionally / Systematically)
8. Have you raised prices in the last 12 months? (No / Yes, for new clients / Yes, across the board)
9. Do you track revenue per employee? (What's that? / Sometimes / Yes, monthly)
10. What's your biggest revenue challenge right now? (Getting clients / Keeping clients / Pricing / Growing existing accounts)

### Results Mapping
| Score Range | Stage | CTA |
|-------------|-------|-----|
| 0–20 | Survival | "You're in the survival stage. The good news: even small pricing changes can unlock big growth. Take the Revenue Health Scorecard →" |
| 20–40 | Growth | "You're growing fast — but pricing leaks are likely costing you. Our audit can find them in 2 weeks." |
| 40–60 | Efficiency | "You have systems but they're underoptimized. This is our sweet spot — we can find 5-figure opportunities hiding in your data." |
| 60–80 | Optimization | "You're doing well, but there's still room to fine-tune. Let's find the gaps." |
| 80–100 | Predictability | "You're ahead of the curve. We can help with the final 20% of optimization." |

## Asset 2: Blog Post — "The 5 Stages of Revenue Health (And Why Most SMBs Are Stuck at Stage 2)"

### Headline
**The 5 Stages of Revenue Health (And Why Most SMBs Are Stuck at Stage 2)**

### Excerpt
Every service business goes through predictable stages of revenue maturity. Here's how to tell where you are — and what it takes to advance to the next level.

### Structure
1. **Introduction**: "Revenue health isn't binary. It's a maturity curve — and most businesses plateau at the same stages."
2. **Stage 1: Survival ($0–$500K)** — Founder does everything, pricing by instinct, no data
3. **Stage 2: Growth ($500K–$2M)** — Growing fast but chaotically, some systems, pricing leaks starting to appear
4. **Stage 3: Efficiency ($2M–$5M)** — Systems in place but underoptimized, data exists but isn't used
5. **Stage 4: Optimization ($5M–$10M)** — Good systems, multiple revenue streams, but missed opportunities
6. **Stage 5: Predictability ($10M+)** — Data-driven, sophisticated, but may have institutional inertia
7. **The key insight**: Most businesses are at a higher stage than their revenue suggests — they just need to unlock it
8. **CTA**: "Not sure where you stand? Take our 5-minute Revenue Health Scorecard →"

## Asset 3: LinkedIn Post Series — "The Four Causal Loops of Revenue"

### Post 1: The Pricing Loop
> Most businesses think revenue is a number. It's actually a system of interconnected loops.
> 
> Here's The Pricing Loop:
> 
> Pricing Strategy → Revenue per Customer → Margins → Investment → Service Quality → Pricing Power
> 
> When it works: Strong pricing → high margins → reinvestment → better service → pricing power strengthens
> 
> When it breaks: Weak pricing → low margins → no reinvestment → service declines → pricing power erodes
> 
> Where does YOUR business fall on this loop?
> 
> #RevenueGrowth #PricingStrategy #BusinessGrowth

### Post 2: The Retention Loop
> The Retention Loop is the most dangerous because it's invisible — until it's too late.
> 
> Customer Experience → Satisfaction → Retention → Revenue Stability → CX Investment → Better CX
> 
> Healthy: Great CX → high retention → stable revenue → invest in CX → even better CX
> 
> Broken: Poor CX → high churn → unstable revenue → cut corners → worse CX → more churn
> 
> The scary part: the "broken" loop can cycle for months before revenue numbers show it.
> 
> Our AI audit catches it early by analyzing engagement patterns as leading indicators.
> 
> #CustomerRetention #Churn #SaaS

### Post 3: The Acquisition Loop
> Most businesses have 3–5 marketing channels but 80% of revenue comes from 1–2.
> 
> The Acquisition Loop:
> Channel Investment → Leads → Conversion → Revenue → Budget → Channel Investment
> 
> The problem: most SMBs can't tell which channels are actually profitable.
> 
> Without multi-touch attribution, you're guessing. And guessing is expensive.
> 
> #MarketingROI #GrowthStrategy #B2B

### Post 4: The Expansion Loop
> The cheapest growth is the growth you already have.
> 
> The Expansion Loop:
> Account Health → Upsell Opportunity → Expansion Revenue → Total Revenue → Account Management → Account Health
> 
> Best-in-class companies generate 35–50% of revenue from existing clients.
> Average: 15–25%.
> 
> The gap isn't lack of opportunity — it's lack of systematic attention.
> 
> #CustomerSuccess #Upsell #RevenueGrowth
