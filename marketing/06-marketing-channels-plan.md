# Marketing Channels Plan v2 — US First 90 Days

## Strategic Context (from external review)

Per the review directives (D4.4), until we have 2–3 real case studies, we run exactly **TWO motions**:

1. **Fractional-CFO / bookkeeper partner channel** (P1 priority)
2. **Founding-client outreach** via LinkedIn + email to the new US Tier 1 list

**PAUSED until case studies exist**: LinkedIn paid ads, SEO/content spend beyond 2 posts/month, all paid events, and any other speculative channel spend. Bank that budget — the constraint is proof, not reach.

**Re-activation triggers**: Resume paused channels when ≥2 published case studies exist.

---

## Motion 1: Fractional-CFO Partner Channel (P1)

### Why This Channel
- **Trust**: CFOs already have the client's confidence and data access
- **Data access**: The CFO already has the books — our #1 bottleneck is solved
- **Alignment**: CFOs see revenue problems but lack AI tools to diagnose them

### Target Partners
- Independent fractional CFOs serving 10–50 person businesses
- Small CPA/bookkeeping firms
- **Paro.io** network members
- **B2B CFO** network members
- Local accounting firms with SMB clients

### The Hook
White-label Revenue Health Scorecard they can give their clients + 15–20% referral fee on any client who buys an audit or retainer.

### Partner Outreach Plan
| Week | Activity | Count |
|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Identify 20 potential partners on LinkedIn | 20 |
| 2 | Send connection requests | 20 |
| 3 | Follow up with scorecard demo offer | 10–15 |
| 4 | Partner calls + first referrals | 5–10 |

### Goal
5 active partners within 60 days.

---

## Motion 2: Founding-Client Outreach (P2)

### The Offer
"We're taking 10 US founding clients. Full Discovery Audit **free** (normally $495–$995), in exchange for: (1) a case study with real numbers (anonymized if preferred), and (2) a testimonial if you're happy."

### Channels
1. **LinkedIn organic post** — Announce the founding-client cohort (Post #1 for the account)
2. **Direct outreach** — 10–20 personalized touches/week to the new US Tier 1 list
3. **Landing page** — `/founding-clients` page on the website (coordinate with Web Developer)

### Outreach Cadence
| Day | Activity | Count |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Mon | Post founding-client announcement (8:30 AM ET) | 1 post |
| Mon–Wed | Research + connect with 10 new Tier 1 accounts | 10 connections |
| Thu–Fri | Follow-up DMs + email to accepted connections | 10 messages |

### Founding-Cohort Tracker
Track in `outreach-tracker.csv` with a "Founding Cohort" column:
- Yes — accepted into the program
- Applied — waiting for data share
- No — doesn't qualify
