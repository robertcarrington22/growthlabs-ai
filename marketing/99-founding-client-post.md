>

>
> Your total time commitment: about 30 minutes. We do the data pull with you.
>
> First 10 qualified businesses. After that it closes.
>
> DM me or comment "interested" and I'll send details. And if you know an owner who's tired of guessing — sending this to them might be the most valuable thing you do for them this year.
