- **Size**: 10–200 employees

## Outreach Notes

- **LinkedIn**: Connect with Founder, CEO, Head of Growth, or Head of Revenue Operations
- **Warm intro**: Prioritize companies with shared connections or alumni networks
- **Value-first**: Lead with the Revenue Health Scorecard (free diagnostic) or our sample blog post
- **Timing**: Start Tier 1 within first 2 weeks, Tier 2 in weeks 3–4, Tier 3 as pipeline allows
- **Tracking**: Use a simple CRM sheet to track outreach dates, responses, and next steps
