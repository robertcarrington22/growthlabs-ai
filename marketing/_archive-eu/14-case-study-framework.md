# Case Study Framework — GrowthLabs AI

## Structure

Every case study follows this 5-section format:

### 1. Client Snapshot (top of page)

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| **Company** | Company Name |
| **Industry** | Digital Agency / SaaS / Consultancy |
| **Size** | 50 employees |
| **Revenue** | €5M |
| **Challenge** | Pricing drift across 200+ clients |
| **Engagement** | Discovery Audit → Growth Retainer |
| **Timeline** | 8 weeks |

### 2. The Problem (2–3 paragraphs)

**What to include**:
- The situation before we started
- What data existed (CRM, billing, analytics)
- What the client *thought* the problem was
- What we discovered once we looked deeper

**Example**:
> "Agency X came to us because they felt their growth had stalled. They were acquiring new clients steadily, but revenue wasn't keeping pace. They suspected pricing was an issue — but they had 200+ clients across 3 service lines, and nobody had time to audit each relationship."
>
> "We connected their CRM, billing system, and project management tools. Within 48 hours, our AI engine had analyzed every client relationship and surfaced a clear picture of where revenue was hiding."

### 3. Our Approach (2–3 paragraphs)

**What to include**:
- How we analyzed the data
- The AI methods used (without being too technical)
- What patterns emerged
- The key insights we presented

**Example**:
> "Our AI engine mapped every client by service type, pricing tier, contract age, and engagement level. It found that clients acquired before 2023 were paying 35% less than comparable new clients — and receiving the same level of service."
>
> "We also identified 12 clients who were using one service but clearly needed a second one (based on support tickets, feature requests, and growth patterns). These were unsolicited upsell opportunities worth €50K+."

### 4. The Results (bullet points, quantified)

**Format**: Bullet points with specific numbers.

**Example**:
- **€240K** in annual upsell revenue identified (and €80K captured in first quarter)
- **22%** increase in pricing consistency across client base
- **15%** reduction in at-risk churn within 30 days of implementing early warning system
- **3 months** payback period on the Discovery Audit fee

**Visual**: Include a before/after comparison if possible.

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Pricing consistency | 62% | 84% | +22% |
| Monthly upsell revenue | €2K | €12K | +500% |
| Client churn (high-risk segment) | 15% | 8% | -47% |

### 5. Client Quote

A direct quote from the client contact. Format:

> *"The most valuable €3K we've spent this year. They found revenue opportunities we'd been walking past for years. The AI analysis caught things our team of 10 people couldn't see."*
>
> — **[Name], [Title] at [Company]**
