# Lead Magnet: Retail Inventory Health Scorecard

## Concept
A free, self-administered 10-question diagnostic for independent retailers and e-commerce brands. Scores the business across inventory health dimensions. Captures leads while delivering immediate actionable insight.

**Goal**: Capture qualified retail leads (name, email, company, revenue range, POS platform) by showing them how much cash is trapped in their stock.

**Distribution**: Gated behind email capture, promoted in retail blog posts, shared via POS reseller partners.

---

## Scorecard Outline (10 Questions, 100 points)

### Dimension 1: Inventory Turnover (25 points)

1. **What are your annual inventory turns?**
   - >6 turns/year = 10 pts
   - 3–6 turns = 7 pts
   - 1–3 turns = 3 pts
   - <1 turn = 0 pts
   - "I don't know" = 2 pts

2. **How does your turnover compare to last year?**
   - Improving = 8 pts
   - Flat = 5 pts
   - Declining = 2 pts
   - Don't track = 0 pts

3. **What's your weeks of cover on hand (average stock / weekly sales)?**
   - <6 weeks = 7 pts
   - 6–12 weeks = 5 pts
   - >12 weeks = 2 pts
   - Don't know = 1 pt

### Dimension 2: Stockout Risk (25 points)

4. **How often do you stock out of your top 20% of SKUs?**
   - Rarely (<1×/quarter) = 10 pts
   - Sometimes (1–3×/quarter) = 7 pts
   - Often (monthly+) = 3 pts
   - Don't track = 2 pts

5. **Do you have a safety stock formula for your best-selling items?**
   - Yes, data-driven = 8 pts
   - Yes, by gut feel = 5 pts
   - No = 0 pts

6. **What percentage of sales comes from items marked down for clearance?**
   - <10% = 7 pts
   - 10–20% = 4 pts
   - >20% = 1 pt
   - Don't know = 2 pts

### Dimension 3: Dead Stock & Carrying Cost (25 points)

7. **What % of your inventory has had zero sales in the last 90 days?**
   - <10% = 10 pts
   - 10–25% = 6 pts
   - >25% = 2 pts
   - Don't know = 3 pts

8. **Do you know your annual carrying cost of inventory (storage, insurance, capital)?**
   - Yes, I've calculated it = 8 pts
   - Rough estimate = 4 pts
   - No = 0 pts

9. **How do you decide what to mark down and when?**
   - Data-driven (sell-through rate, age) = 7 pts
   - Gut feel + calendar = 4 pts
   - Only when cash-flow forces it = 1 pt

### Dimension 4: Seasonal Planning (25 points)

10. **How do you determine seasonal/holiday buy quantities?**
    - Data-driven forecast (historical sales + trend) = 10 pts
   - Last year's sales adjusted by gut = 6 pts
   - Supplier recommendation = 3 pts
   - Gut feel only = 0 pts

---

### Scoring

| Score | Status | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 80–100 | Inventory Healthy | Fine-tuning recommendations |
| 60–79 | Moderate Gaps | 2–3 high-impact inventory improvements |
| 40–59 | Significant Leaks | Priority inventory audit recommended |
| 0–39 | Critical | Immediate inventory intervention needed |
