## Account 1: Focus Lab — VERIFIED ✅

- **What they do:** Global B2B brand identity agency (brand strategy, identity, design systems)
- **HQ:** Savannah, GA · **Size:** ~20 people · **Founded:** 2010
- **Decision maker:** **Bill Kenney — Co-Founder & CEO** (active on LinkedIn, Dribbble, Behance; posts frequently about agency life and B2B brand)
- **Notable:** Founded "at a dusty Applebee's booth" in 2010 (his own origin story — he tells it publicly). Clients include Netflix, Marketo, Braze, Frame.io. They rebranded themselves and wrote about it.
- **Why they fit:** Classic ICP — founder-led, project-based revenue, premium positioning. Agencies this size almost never have project-level profitability data.
- **Personalization hooks:**
  - His public origin story (Applebee's booth → global B2B brand agency)
  - Agencies that sell "premium brand" often underprice their own work — the shoemaker's-children angle
  - He publishes about running the agency, so a peer-to-peer builder-to-builder tone fits
- **Angle:** Founding-client offer, direct. He's a builder who bets on other builders.
