## POST #3 — Friday, 8:30 AM ET

**Slot:** Retail/inventory flag plant — GMROI walkthrough
**Source:** `21-retail-content-posts.md`, Post 1, condensed. The worked example is hypothetical and framed that way — no client claims.

### Copy-paste post

> Some of what's in your stockroom isn't inventory. It's expensive clutter.
>
> If you run an independent shop, there's one number that tells you the difference — and most retailers have never calculated it: GMROI. In plain English: for every dollar sitting in your stockroom, how many dollars of gross profit does it earn you per year?
>
> The math is one line:
>
> GMROI = gross profit ÷ average inventory (at cost)
>
> Say you run a boutique doing $200,000 a year at a 50% margin. That's $100,000 in gross profit. Your average inventory, at cost, is $80,000.
>
> $100,000 ÷ $80,000 = **$1.25**
>
> Every dollar on your shelves earns you $1.25 a year. Retail benchmarks put healthy GMROI at $2.00 or better — below $1.50, your inventory is working against you, tying up cash you could use for marketing, payroll, or simply breathing room.
>
> Where does it hide? Usually four places: stock that hasn't sold in 90+ days, months of cover on slow movers while best sellers run out, 20 versions of an item when 5 would do, and last season's gut-feel buy still on the shelf in February.
>
> Your POS system already has everything needed to find it.
>
> Want to know where you stand? Free scorecard, 3 minutes:
>
> growthlabs-ai.vercel.app/scorecard
